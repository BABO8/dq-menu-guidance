Warning: Avoid Third-Party Coupon Scams

Websites offering "printable 50% off Dairy Queen coupons" or "coupon codes" are virtually always unauthorized or fraudulent. Dairy Queen registers do not accept generic text discount codes; valid digital discounts must be scanned via QR code directly within the official DQ® app.
