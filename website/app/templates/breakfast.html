Does Dairy Queen Serve Breakfast? Hours & Menu Overview

A frequent question among fast-food travelers is: Does Dairy Queen serve breakfast? The answer is yes, but exclusively at participating regional locations — primarily throughout Texas (under the famed Texas Country Foods banner) and select Southern highway travel plazas. In contrast, standard Northern and Midwest Dairy Queen locations do not serve morning breakfast and open between 10:00 AM and 11:00 AM with the lunch menu.

Crucial Regional Notice: Most Northern Locations Do NOT Serve Breakfast

Nationwide corporate standards do not mandate morning breakfast service. Unless you are visiting a Texas Dairy Queen (Texas Country Foods) or a participating Southern franchise along major highway corridors, stores generally open between 10:30 AM and 11:00 AM with the lunch menu.

What Are Dairy Queen Breakfast Hours?

At participating breakfast locations:

  • Monday – Saturday: 6:00 AM to 10:30 AM
  • Sunday: 7:00 AM to 11:00 AM
  • Lunch Transition: At 10:30 AM sharp, grills transition to burgers and chicken baskets.

How to Verify Before Driving

Always check the official DQ app or call your destination store directly. Select your store on the map; if breakfast items appear under the morning menu tab, breakfast is served.
