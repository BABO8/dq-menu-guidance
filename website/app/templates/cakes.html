What is a Treatzza Pizza®?

Originally debuted in the 1990s and revived by popular customer demand, the Treatzza Pizza® is a frozen dessert pizza. Built upon a crunchy chocolate cookie fudge crust, topped with smooth vanilla soft serve, and dressed with candy pieces (such as Choco Brownie or Reese's® peanut butter cups), it comes pre-sliced into 8 triangular slices for casual parties.

How to Order & Customize

If you need a cake immediately, you can walk into nearly any DQ Grill & Chill or Treat store and select an undecorated round cake from the retail display freezer. The counter crew will pipe personalized names and celebratory messages (e.g. "Happy 10th Birthday Leo!") at no extra charge within 5 minutes.

OREO® Blizzard Cake — DQ's Most Popular Custom Flavor

The OREO® Blizzard Cake is DQ's best-selling customized cake variety, available in both 8-inch ($32.99–$38.99) and 10-inch ($39.99–$44.99) formats. It layers creamy vanilla soft serve with crushed OREO® cookie pieces blended throughout, sits on a chocolate soft-serve base, and includes the signature fudge & crunch center — all draped in white whipped frosting with OREO® cookie garnishes. Allow 24–48 hours advance notice when ordering a custom OREO® Blizzard Cake.

Custom DQ Birthday Cakes & How to Order Online

Dairy Queen offers two convenient ordering paths for custom birthday ice cream cakes:

  • In-Store Walk-In: Visit any DQ® Grill & Chill location and choose from ready-decorated display cakes. The team can pipe a personalized name or message at no charge in about 5 minutes.
  • Online Pre-Order: Use the official DQ app or the DQ website's store locator to call your local store and pre-order a custom cake up to 72 hours in advance. Online direct checkout for cakes is available at select franchise groups.
  • Cupcake Calories & Portions: Individual DQ ice cream cupcakes typically contain 280–420 calories per serving depending on crunch layer and fudge drizzle — offering portion-controlled celebration treats.
