What Made the 2022 Stackburger Redesign Different?

Prior to 2022, Dairy Queen's burgers used standard grill press cooking that occasionally resulted in dried patties. The Signature Stackburger platform instituted thicker, freshly seasoned 1/6 lb ground beef patties, melted dual cheeses placed strategically between meat layers to trap heat, and toasted artisan-style buns with higher butter content.
