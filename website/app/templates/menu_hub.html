Important Pricing & Availability Notice

Dairy Queen locations are over 99% independently franchised. Retail prices, coupon acceptance, and product availability vary significantly between locations. Food items (burgers and chicken) are served at DQ® Grill & Chill® restaurants, whereas traditional DQ® Treat stores serve only soft-serve desserts and cakes. Check your local restaurant register or the official DQ app to confirm final checkout amounts.
