Grill & Chill vs. Treat Store Availability

Hot food items (chicken strips, burgers, fries, onion rings) are served exclusively at DQ® Grill & Chill® and "Brazier" franchise locations with commercial fryers and grills. Mall counters and standalone Treat-only kiosks do not prepare hot food.
