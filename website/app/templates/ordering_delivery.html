Third-Party Delivery Markup Notice

Prices for Dairy Queen items listed on DoorDash, Uber Eats, and Grubhub are set by franchisees and typically carry a 15% to 25% markup over in-store counter prices, in addition to delivery fees, service charges, and driver gratuity.

Can Ice Cream Cakes Be Delivered?

While some DoorDash and Uber Eats listings include standard 8-inch round cakes from the walk-in display freezer, delivery drivers transport orders in standard vehicles that lack commercial sub-zero refrigeration. Because Dairy Queen soft serve begins losing structural integrity at temperatures above 25°F, ordering celebration cakes through third-party couriers during summer months carries high risk of melting.

Our Recommendation: Pre-order your cake online via the official Dairy Queen cake portal (dairyqueen.com) and pick it up personally with an insulated cooler bag.
