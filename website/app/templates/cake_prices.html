The 8-Inch vs. 10-Inch Value Math

A standard 8-inch cake provides approximately 50 square inches of surface area, yielding 8 generous slices. In contrast, the 10-inch round cake offers roughly 78.5 square inches—a 57% increase in dessert volume for an average price increase of just \$5.00 to \$7.00.

If you are hosting 8 or more guests, upgrading to the 10-inch reduces your cost per serving from roughly \$3.10 down to \$2.45 and guarantees seconds for hungry attendees.

Customization & Personalization Fees

Hand-Piped Writing: Free at virtually all DQ Grill & Chill and Treat locations. Even on walk-in freezer case purchases, counter staff will pipe names and celebratory phrases ("Happy Birthday Jackson!") in colored gel at no additional charge.

Edible Photo Prints: Many stores charge a \$5.00 to \$8.00 setup fee to scan a personal photograph or print licensed Disney, Marvel, or sports team edible wafer graphics.

How to Order Dairy Queen Cakes for Maximum Value

1. Immediate Need: Walk-in Case

If you need a cake in under 10 minutes, visit the retail display freezer. Stores stock 4–8 pre-decorated 8" and 10" cakes ready to write on immediately.

2. Custom Orders: DQcakes.com

Use the official DQcakes.com portal to select your local store, customize the Blizzard layer (e.g., OREO® or Butterfinger®), pick border colors, and set a pickup time.

3. Notice Requirements

Blizzard custom cakes and 11" x 14" sheet cakes require a minimum of 24 to 48 hours advance notice so kitchen crews can freeze layers solid before decorating.
