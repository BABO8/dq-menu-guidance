The History of the Dilly® Bar

Invented in 1955 at a Dairy Queen in Moorhead, Minnesota, when a franchisee poured soft serve onto a paper plate, inserted a stick, and dunked it in cold chocolate coating, the Dilly® Bar has become an American fast-food icon. Today, Dilly Bars are sold individually and in discounted 6-pack take-home boxes in Chocolate, Cherry, Butterscotch, and Non-Dairy Vegan varieties.

Dairy Queen Dipped Cones (Chocolate & Cherry)

Dairy Queen Dipped Cones take DQ's world-famous vanilla soft serve and instantly flash-freeze it in a warm flavored candy shell that hardens into a crisp crackable coating.

  • Chocolate Dipped Cone: Available year-round nationwide in Small ($3.19), Medium ($3.79), and Large ($4.39). Ranging from 320 to 650 calories.
  • Cherry Dipped Cone: A vibrant red, sweet cherry coating with a nostalgic fruity crunch, rotating seasonally at select franchised stores.
  • Seasonal Dips: Locations occasionally feature Churro, Butterscotch, or Confetti Cake dips during limited-time spring and summer windows.

Soft Serve Ice Cream Ingredients at Dairy Queen

DQ soft serve is legally categorized by the FDA as "ice milk" or "reduced-fat ice cream" because it contains roughly 5% butterfat (traditional commercial ice cream requires a minimum 10% milkfat). Core ingredients include milkfat and nonfat milk, sugar, corn syrup, whey, mono and diglycerides, artificial flavor, guar gum, polysorbate 80, carrageenan, and vitamin A palmitate.

Dairy-Free & Gluten-Free Options

Guests looking for dairy-free treats can enjoy the pre-packaged Non-Dairy Dilly® Bar (crafted with coconut cream and chocolate coating, certified vegan, and gluten-free) or fruit-flavored Starkiss® Bars. Because open soft-serve machines and malt powders contain gluten and milk proteins, sealed novelties offer the safest protection against cross-contact.
