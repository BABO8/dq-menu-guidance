Seasonal Flavor Tracker

Current & Seasonal Dairy Queen Blizzard® Flavors

Dairy Queen introduces new Blizzard menus every spring/summer and autumn/winter. Here is your verified tracker for active limited-time offers, Flavor of the Month rotations, and seasonal return windows.

Seasonal rotations bring back popular caramel nut clusters, rich brownies, and summery fruit cheesecake blends.

Seasonal Availability Note

Limited-time flavors depend strictly on supplier inventory at independent franchise stores. Once a store depletes its seasonal cookie/candy shipment, the item is discontinued until its next promotional cycle.

How Does the "Blizzard of the Month" Work?

Since 2003, American Dairy Queen Corporation has designated a featured "Flavor of the Month" for each calendar month. Participating stores advertise the featured flavor prominently on drive-thru menu boards and in the official mobile app, frequently offering bonus DQ Rewards points to app members during promotional launch weeks.
