Why Do Blizzard Prices Vary by Location?

Because Dairy Queen franchises are individually owned, pricing reflects local market dynamics. A franchise operating in Manhattan, Hawaii, or an airport terminal incurs significantly higher commercial rent and labor overhead than a location in suburban Missouri or rural Texas. Additionally, Royal Blizzards (which feature a core filled with fudge or fruit) generally cost \$0.30 to \$0.50 more than traditional Candy Classics.
