How We Research & Fact-Check Our Dairy Queen Guides

Fast-food restaurant menus and franchise pricing evolve rapidly. Every guide published in this library is crafted by our dedicated U.S. menu research team using primary sources, including:

  • Official Corporate Documentation: American Dairy Queen Corporation nutritional disclosure PDFs, press releases, and SEC franchise disclosure documents.
  • Store-Level Receipt Verification: In-store visits and drive-thru receipts gathered across low-cost (Texas, Ohio), average (Florida, Arizona), and high-cost (California, New York) markets.
  • Government Standards: U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) menu labeling guidelines (21 CFR 101.11) and FALCPA allergen mandates.
